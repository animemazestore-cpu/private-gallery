# Project Requirements

## Product
Private Gallery: a web-based photo vault/gallery for a small trusted audience.

## Core user stories

1. A visitor opens the website and sees a locked screen.
2. The visitor enters the secret PIN.
3. If authorized, the visitor sees the private gallery.
4. The visitor can browse and view photos comfortably on a phone.
5. The visitor does not need to download/store the photos permanently.
6. The administrator can sign in separately.
7. The administrator can upload photos.
8. The administrator can delete photos.
9. Protected photos are not publicly accessible.

## Out of scope for MVP

- Public accounts/registration.
- Social features.
- Comments/likes.
- Public sharing links.
- Video support unless explicitly added later.
- Complex roles beyond visitor/admin.

## Quality goals

Privacy, simplicity, mobile usability, low maintenance, secure defaults, and easy deployment.
